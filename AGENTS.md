- The covenant preserves their proper relationship through stewardship.
- The shrine is a regulator/alignment point, never a monster prison.
- The Entity is an independent ancient Unseen intelligence attracted to breaches; the breach does not create it.
- Attention creates proximity; perception across the boundary is reciprocal.
- The Entity's perceptible body is failed translation, not a literal spider body.
- Solomon and his sister held complementary procedural/perceptual functions.
- Succession requires recognized capacity, transmitted knowledge, and voluntary acceptance.
- Solomon physically returned to Calabar for maintenance; Bode was not his maintenance proxy.
- Malik has procedural aptitude but is not chosen.
- Kai has perceptual aptitude but is not chosen.
- Ade has no supernatural office.
- Bode has limited family-history/boundary knowledge, not full mythology.
- Eno is household/family authority, not mythology expert.
- The sister's full death causality remains withheld in the current screenplay.
- The inner maintained door belongs to the same covenant system and remains closed/unexplained through Sequence X.

## Cultural restraint
