# EKPO — AUTHORITATIVE CANONICAL MYTHOLOGY

Status: LOCKED / FOUNDATIONAL
Effective: 2026-08-13
Authority: Latest explicit creator instruction

This document supersedes prior working mythology wherever those materials conflict with it. It is a repository-level canon source, not a screenplay scene and not permission to invent additional mythology.

## 1. Core architecture

EKPO is a psychological supernatural family drama about inheritance.

- Family is the subject.
- Grief is the doorway.
- Inheritance is the engine.
- The supernatural is the language.

The governing principle is:

> Reality survives not through power, but through stewardship.

The story is not about defeating an evil monster. It is about a family inheriting responsibility for maintaining a relationship between realities, and the human damage caused when that responsibility is preserved as procedure but withheld as knowledge.

## 2. The Seen and the Unseen

Reality consists of two complementary conditions.

### The Seen

The material human world, governed by stable matter, sequence, physical distance, bodily identity, and ordinary causality.

### The Unseen

A broader metaphysical ecology. It is not merely a world of the dead and must never become a generic fantasy dimension or demon realm.

It may contain ancestors, spiritual intelligences, dream beings, primordial presences, forces, beings beyond human categories, and phenomena humans may not meaningfully distinguish from beings.

The Unseen is not evil and is not inferior. It operates according to different conditions of existence.

## 3. The covenant

The covenant preserves the proper relationship between the Seen and the Unseen.

It is not:

- a bargain with the Entity;
- a demonic contract;
- a prison mechanism;
- a monster-containment pact.

It is inherited stewardship. Its purpose is equilibrium between realities. Neither condition should overwhelm the other.

## 4. The shrine

The shrine is not a prison, cage, monster room, or place where the Entity lives.

It is:

- a regulator;
- a point of alignment;
- a physical locus where the relationship between Seen and Unseen is maintained.

The red bindings, brass talisman, knots, materials, intervals, measurements, environmental observations, and physical maintenance belong to this regulatory system.

Kai did not release a trapped monster. He disrupted an existing regulator.

## 5. The Entity

The Entity is an independent, ancient intelligence native to the Unseen that is naturally attracted to breaches in the equilibrium between Seen and Unseen.

The breach does not create it.

The breach attracts it.

It is not:

- Solomon's sister;
- a ghost;
- a demon;
- a god;
- the ruler of the Unseen;
- the purpose of the covenant;
- something trapped inside the shrine;
- something summoned by Kai;
- a manifestation generated by imbalance;
- merely a symptom produced by imbalance.

It existed before the Oku family and may be vastly older than their understanding of it. It may be one of a category of Unseen intelligences drawn toward instability, but no taxonomy is currently canon. Do not over-classify it.

## 6. Why it comes

The current causal chain is:

1. Solomon dies.
2. Established custodial continuity is lost.
3. Kai leaves the maintained path.
4. Kai disrupts the shrine's red binding.
5. Kai removes the brass talisman.
6. The regulator destabilizes.
7. The breach produces metaphysical pressure.
8. The Entity detects that instability.
9. It approaches the breach.
10. Kai perceives it.
11. Perception creates reciprocity.
12. The Entity increasingly becomes aware that Kai can perceive it.

The Entity is not hunting Kai in a conventional predator sense. From Kai's point of view, its increasing attention may feel exactly like hunting.

## 7. Recognition across generations

A previous catastrophic rupture brought the same Entity, or the same ancient intelligence, into contact with Solomon's sister. That encounter left an impression or metaphysical scar.

When the new breach forms, the Entity is drawn back toward the same regulatory node.

Kai's perceptual sensitivity resembles his aunt's. This is not simplistic magical blood. Kai interacts with the Unseen in a similar way.

The writers' room logic is that the Entity encounters in Kai a shape, frequency, or pattern of consciousness it has encountered before. Do not reduce this to human-style family recognition.

## 8. Failed translation physicality

The Entity's terrifying perceptible form is not necessarily its true body.

When something native to the Unseen becomes perceptible in the Seen, the Seen attempts to impose anatomy, sequence, spatial coherence, mass, distance, and bodily continuity onto something that does not naturally obey those conditions.

The result is a failed translation.

Permitted visual logic includes:

- extreme height;
- too many articulations;
- impossible joint logic;
- unstable proportions;
- anatomy that almost resolves and then fails;
- movement that does not fully obey continuity;
- spatially wrong parts;
- increasing legibility as the breach worsens.

Do not reduce this to a big spider monster. Spider imagery may rhyme with articulation, but the Entity is not literally a spider god.

## 9. Attention rule

Perception across the boundary is reciprocal.

Internal rule:

> Attention creates proximity.

To perceive the Unseen is, in some degree, to approach it. Answering a voice, following a shape, acknowledging a presence, or repeatedly observing a phenomenon may increase reciprocal awareness.

This is not a simple summoning mechanic. It is a rule of metaphysical relationship.

Kai is especially vulnerable because he can notice what others cannot.

## 10. Two-function stewardship

Solomon and his sister were not competing successors. They were a complementary pair.

The covenant's stewardship historically functioned through two interdependent roles. Do not invent final ritual titles yet.

### Procedural / Seen-facing function

Solomon held the procedural role. His responsibilities included:

- maintaining the shrine;
- preserving physical alignment;
- renewing bindings;
- knot systems;
- materials;
- intervals;
- measurements;
- records;
- environmental conditions;
- accumulated procedural knowledge;
- physical integrity of the regulator.

His aptitude was discipline, structure, maintenance, repetition, logistics, record keeping, and protection through systems.

### Perceptual / Unseen-facing function

Solomon's sister held the perceptual role. Her responsibilities included:

- perceiving pressure from the Unseen;
- interpreting manifestations;
- noticing environmental changes;
- distinguishing meaningful disturbance from noise;
- understanding how attention changes contact;
- documenting perceptual phenomena;
- recognizing similar perceptual sensitivity in future potential successors;
- helping prepare that successor.

She did not identify destiny. She recognized capacity.

## 11. Succession

Succession is not prophecy, supernatural appointment, or automatic inheritance.

The system is:

1. Capacity is recognized.
2. Knowledge is transmitted.
3. Responsibility is voluntarily accepted.

The perceptual custodian traditionally helps recognize perceptual sensitivity. The procedural custodian preserves and transmits practical maintenance knowledge.

Neither function is sufficient alone.

Blood does not remove agency.

## 12. Solomon's original failure

In young adulthood Solomon failed his procedural responsibility.

The exact mundane act is intentionally unresolved. It should eventually feel human and preventable: delayed maintenance, leaving when he should have remained, ignoring an interval, or assuming the work could wait are the correct scale of failure, but none is final canon yet.

Locked consequence:

- Solomon failed his side of the partnership.
- The boundary destabilized.
- His sister was forced to act without the protection/support Solomon's function should have provided.
- She restored equilibrium.
- She died doing so.

## 13. Reveal strategy for the sister's death

The full causal truth is canonical backstory, but it must not be explained early on-page.

The red-corded-room sequence should reveal only that:

- she worked alongside Solomon;
- she kept a different kind of record;
- their records overlap on the same dates;
- her handwriting eventually stops;
- Solomon's continues alone.

The fact that Solomon's failure contributed to her death must be reserved for a later moment when it directly changes Malik's present decision, ideally when Malik is in danger of repeating Solomon's mistake.

## 14. Solomon's wrong lesson

After his sister dies, Solomon learns the wrong lesson.

He does not conclude that no one should carry the work alone.

He concludes:

> No one will ever suffer because I fail again.

He becomes obsessive. He maintains his side meticulously, studies his sister's records, attempts to compensate for her missing function, isolates himself, and tries to perform both halves.

He is never naturally as capable on the perceptual side as she was.

His later records may contain uncertain, awkward attempts to describe phenomena she once recorded confidently.

## 15. Solomon's true failure

Solomon's ultimate failure is transmission and succession.

He preserves the work.

He fails to preserve the partnership.

He protects the covenant while failing to prepare the next generation. His secrecy is tragic protection/control that leaves his sons vulnerable.

## 16. Solomon and Canada

The superseded model in which Bode routinely performed Solomon's custodial maintenance in Calabar is not canon.

Solomon physically returned to Calabar when maintenance required his presence.

His sons may have understood these trips as property matters, funerals, family obligations, or ordinary visits. Later records can reveal that the travel corresponded with maintenance cycles.

## 17. Bode knowledge boundary

Bode is not a co-custodian, maintenance proxy, or mythology expert.

He knows fragments of family history, the land, dangerous boundaries, consequences surrounding his sister's death, Solomon's changed behavior, practical warnings, enough to know the shrine should not be disturbed, and enough to recognize patterns when the nephews encounter them.

He does not know the full cosmology, every rule of the Unseen, complete succession architecture, all of Solomon's records, all of the sister's perceptual records, or how to replace either custodian.

His arc may move from participating in Solomon's silence toward finally allowing the nephews access to what Solomon withheld. He must not become an exposition machine.

## 18. Malik

Malik is naturally suited to Solomon's procedural stewardship function because of who he already is, not because he is cosmically chosen.

His psychology centers on control, order, maintenance, prevention, responsibility, checking, structure, and fighting entropy.

His immature form is:

> I protect Kai by controlling Kai.

His mature form is:

> I protect Kai by maintaining the conditions that allow Kai to do his own work.

This is central to his arc.

## 19. Kai

Kai is naturally suited to the perceptual function formerly held by Solomon's sister.

He is not a Chosen One.

His aptitude is pattern recognition, sensitivity, altered perception, noticing what others miss, proximity to boundaries, and the ability to perceive Unseen pressure.

His aunt likely would have recognized and trained this capacity had she lived.

Because she died and Solomon failed succession, Kai discovers the capacity in the worst possible way: by crossing a boundary without understanding it.

His curiosity is not stupidity. His mistake is ignorance produced by inherited silence.

## 20. Ade

Ade has no invented supernatural office.

Do not create a third metaphysical role for symmetry.

His importance remains human and relational. He keeps emotional connection alive, uses humor as protection and social mediation, sees relational patterns the others miss, may become the person capable of noticing when stewardship consumes Malik and Kai, and embodies the truth that belonging does not require a mystical function.

## 21. Eno

Eno is family and household authority.

She may recognize danger, fear, family patterns, Solomon's secrecy, Bode's behavior, and the need to protect the household.

She does not possess full shrine knowledge, covenant cosmology, Entity taxonomy, succession mechanics, or perceptual-role expertise.

She must not become a second Bode.

## 22. Inner maintained door

The inner door inside the maintenance room belongs to the same covenant system.

It is not a second unrelated portal, monster cage, place where the Entity is trapped, or competing mythology.

It is a human-facing threshold/interface within the same regulatory architecture. Its full function remains unrevealed.

Visual facts:

- it is maintained;
- measured;
- bound;
- periodically worked;
- one section may be unfinished after Solomon's death.

Do not make it move supernaturally. Do not stage a scare behind it during the red-room reveal.

## 23. Red-corded-room reveal

The room is a spiritual maintenance workshop. It should reveal evidence, patterns, responsibility, and generational structure — not a cosmology lecture.

Material may include, where culturally and research appropriate: chalk, raffia, herbs, knots, maps, diagrams, brass pieces, repaired drums, measurements, ledgers, tools, records, environmental tracking, and old/modern working materials.

Avoid random occult decoration, unexplained bones, generic ley-line vocabulary, and decorative ritual clutter.

### Two-ledger reveal

Solomon's records are procedural, physical, exact, measurable, and maintenance-oriented.

His sister's records are perceptual, observational, environmental, experiential, and related to manifestations and attention.

Their records share dates. The audience discovers partnership through overlap before anyone explains it.

Do not use unearned role labels such as Keeper, Witness, Guardian, or Intercessor.

### Solomon's later records

After the sister's notes stop, Solomon's procedural records continue alone. Later entries begin to contain uncertain sensory observations, tentative descriptions, questions, and awkward attempts to document phenomena she once described confidently.

One or two strong examples are enough.

### Character geometry

- Malik gravitates toward Solomon's procedural records.
- Kai recognizes recent experiences in the sister's perceptual records.
- Ade notices the pattern forming between his brothers.
- Bode confirms very little.

Key image: Malik with Solomon's knot work; Kai with the aunt's observational notebook; Ade seeing both.

No one appoints them. The audience sees the pattern first.

The domestic supernatural escalation must be written fresh from the current canonical screenplay state after the river.

Requirements:

- the brothers remain physically together;
- no isolated one-brother-at-a-time scare vignettes;

- the experience evolves the river's established grammar;
- no giant spider shadow;
- no generic possession;
- no ash/bell exorcism mechanics;

This disturbance makes the red-corded-room discovery inevitable.

## 25. Entity/aunt rhyme in the red room

The sister's notebook may contain observations that rhyme with Kai's recent experience: insects stopping, environmental stillness, pressure at the neck, altered distance, a tall unresolved form, and articulated geometry that refuses stable anatomy.

Do not state on-page that it is definitely the same Entity. The audience should infer a prior encounter.

Writer-level canon: the previous rupture brought this intelligence into contact with the sister; the current breach draws it back.

## 26. Motif canon

### Red thread / cord

Meaning: restraint, maintenance, agreement, stewardship, boundary made physical.

It is not generic magical rope.

### Brass

Meaning: continuity, inheritance, handled material, regulatory instrument.

Not every brass object is supernatural.

### Spider

Keep the small shrine spider as ecological witness and possible visual rhyme with articulation.

Do not literalize a spider deity, repeat giant spider imagery, or make spider anatomy the Entity's zoological identity.

### Thresholds / doors

Include shrine recess, red-corded room, inner maintained door, path, ravine, and river boundary.

Meaning: maintained separation, responsibility, crossing, permeability.

### Sound

Use river silence, insect withdrawal, altered sound, voice/recognition, and perception under pressure. Sound must remain exact and causal, not decorative.

## 30. Final operating rule

Do not simplify EKPO into "two brothers inherit magic jobs and a monster comes through a portal."

The canonical ecosystem is:

A family inherited responsibility for maintaining the relationship between Seen and Unseen. That responsibility historically required two complementary forms of stewardship. Solomon failed his side, his sister died compensating, and he spent the rest of his life preserving the work while failing to transmit it. His sons return unprepared. Kai breaks the regulator. An ancient intelligence in the Unseen detects the breach. Malik and Kai begin to mirror the two functions their father failed to preserve, while Ade remains the human brother capable of seeing what the inheritance is doing to them.
