# EKPO Canonical Mythology Refactor -- Repository Update

## Purpose

This document records the canonical mythology refactor for EKPO. It
supersedes previous working assumptions regarding the shrine, the
Entity, Solomon's history, and the metaphysical structure of the story.

This is a repository-level architectural change.

The screenplay is no longer about defeating an evil force.

It is about inheriting responsibility for maintaining reality's balance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Objective

Replace the previous "monster imprisoned in a shrine" interpretation
with a unified covenant cosmology.

The supernatural system must support:

- Inheritance
- Stewardship
- Forgotten responsibility
- Boundaries
- Generational failure

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Canonical Cosmology

## Reality

Reality consists of two complementary domains.

### The Seen

The ordinary material world inhabited by humanity.

### The Unseen

The Unseen is **not** merely the land of the dead.

It is a broader metaphysical order containing:

- Ancestors
- Spiritual intelligences
- Dream beings
- Primordial presences
- Archetypal forces
- Entities beyond human categorization

Some are benevolent.

Some are dangerous.

Most simply exist according to their own nature.

The Unseen is necessary.

It is not evil.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Covenant

An ancient covenant preserves the proper relationship between the Seen
and the Unseen.

The covenant is **not** an agreement with a monster.

It is humanity's inherited responsibility to preserve the proper
separation between realities.

Reality survives because boundaries survive.

The covenant protects **both** worlds.

Neither world should consume the other.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Shrine

The shrine is **not** a prison, magical cage, or demon trap.

It is a point of alignment, a regulator, and a physical locus where the
covenant is maintained.

The Oku family inherited stewardship of this node.

They are custodians---not chosen heroes, priests, or rulers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Red Binding

The red cords maintain alignment.

Removing them weakens the covenant.

## The Brass Talisman

The brass talisman maintains equilibrium.

Removing it destabilizes reality's balance rather than releasing a
prisoner.

## The Entity

The Entity is not imprisoned, not the source of evil, and not the
purpose of the covenant.

It is one manifestation of imbalance.

When reality falls out of alignment, aspects of the Unseen become
perceptible within the Seen.

The Entity is the first catastrophic visible expression of that
fracture.

It is a symptom, not the disease.

# Canonical Theme

> Reality survives not through power, but through stewardship.

The horror emerges from forgotten responsibility, not evil.

# Solomon

Solomon spent his life maintaining equilibrium.

His defining characteristic is discipline born from guilt.

He regarded himself as paying both an inherited debt and a personal one.

He continued maintaining the covenant after emigrating to Canada because
responsibility cannot be escaped.

# Solomon's Sister

Canonical history:

- Solomon and his sister were both raised as potential custodians.
- She possessed greater spiritual intuition.
- Solomon became complacent.
- A required act of stewardship was neglected.
- The resulting imbalance began manifesting.
- His sister restored equilibrium.
- She died restoring the covenant.

She did not die because she broke the rules.

She died because Solomon failed his responsibility.

# Solomon's Greatest Failure

His greatest failure was failing to prepare his sons.

He preserved the covenant.

He failed succession.

Silence became another broken boundary.

# Kai

Kai repeats Solomon's philosophical mistake.

He assumes an old object cannot matter.

His ignorance---not rebellion---breaks continuity.

History repeats.

# Horror Escalation

1. Subtle imbalance
2. Atmospheric distortion
3. Dreams, memory anomalies, animal behaviour, spatial wrongness
4. Manifestation of the Entity
5. Increasing permeability between the Seen and the Unseen

Avoid introducing unrelated supernatural mechanics.
