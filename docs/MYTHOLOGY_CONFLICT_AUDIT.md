## Current Screenplay Result

The executed screenplay is compatible with the covenant cosmology after one
mechanical image correction in Sequence VI.

### Sequence VI - The Land and the Shrine

Previous implication:

- roots appeared to hold the shrine shut, which could preserve prison imagery.

Canonical correction:

- roots now brace the shrine's seams.

The scene's dramatic intent remains unchanged. Kai intentionally breaks the red
binding, removes the equilibrium talisman, and chooses not to restore it. This
breaks alignment; it does not release a prisoner.

No dialogue, character choice, scene order, horror beat, or later executed
sequence required rewriting during this pass.

## Earlier Full-Draft Material Requiring Creative Redevelopment

These scenes are not present in the current canonical screenplay. They must not
be copied forward mechanically.

### Hidden Shrine - approximately page 11

Conflict:

- prison-coded root imagery;
- pre-refactor chosen/compelled logic surrounding Kai;
- shrine discovery treated as the opening of a contained threat.

Required future principle:

- retain Kai's curiosity and culpability;
- treat the binding and talisman as alignment and equilibrium mechanisms.

### Kitchen and Red-Thread Room - approximately pages 20-23

Conflict:

- Solomon described as "chosen";
- the force described as wanting balance;
- mythology framed as a contest between rigid and permissive containment.

Required future principle:

- Solomon and his sister were potential custodians, not chosen heroes;
- the Entity manifests imbalance and is not the covenant's corrective purpose;
- the dramatic conflict must concern stewardship, truth, consent, and failed
  succession.

### Mama Ima / Three Cords - approximately pages 25-27

Conflict:

- ritual exposition assigns competing containment philosophies;
- an external lore authority resolves the system too directly;
- older Emeka mythology governs the family wound.

Required future principle:

- recover only playable family conflict;
- derive knowledge from custodial labor, incomplete records, and earned human
  testimony;
- do not restore Emeka or a mythology oracle.

### Solomon Notebook and Cassette - approximately pages 47-48 and 56-57

Conflict:

- Solomon is positioned as the enlightened opponent of Bode's containment;
- recordings explain the supernatural system;
- the shrine's purpose is debated as degrees of sealing.

Required future principle:

- preserve Solomon as a successful but guilt-driven steward whose greatest
  failure was succession;
- keep the covenant larger than Solomon's personal philosophy;
- reveal mythology only when it creates a harder family choice.

### Ravine, Shrine Chamber, and Trials - approximately pages 67-74

Conflict:

- Emeka-return mythology;
- burial hollows and "three returned / three remain" present the Entity as a
  central adversary demanding people;
- the shrine functions as a contained subterranean monster space;
- cracking the talisman operates as victory.

Required future principle:

- rebuild rather than line-edit;
- the shrine remains a regulator and point of alignment;
- the talisman maintains equilibrium and cannot be destroyed as a generic
  defeat mechanism;
- any trial must arise from increasing permeability between the Seen and
  Unseen and force a stewardship choice.

### Escape and Ending Reversal - approximately pages 74-81

Conflict:

- escape from the Entity substitutes for restoration of alignment;
- talisman fragments and reassembly treat the object as a monster key;
- the ending centers surviving a villain rather than inheriting
  responsibility.

Required future principle:

- redesign the climax and ending around responsibility for both realities;
- preserve brotherly choice only where it produces a credible covenant
  consequence;
- do not imply that defeating or trapping the Entity resolves the fracture.

## Unresolved Canon Questions

These are intentionally unresolved and are not contradictions:

- the culturally grounded name of the covenant node or regulator;
- ritual and maintenance vocabulary requiring Efik/Calabar specialist review;
- the sister's name;
- the exact practical act, location, and witnesses involved when she restored
  equilibrium;
- what Bode and Eno know about that event;
- the safe repair or realignment procedure;
- the definitive relationship between the small shrine spider and the Entity;
- the dramatic timing of the sister's death revelation;
- how far Entity learning and agency extend while it remains a manifestation
  of imbalance.

No future scene may answer these questions through invention or by restoring
superseded material from the earlier PDFs.

## Validation Rule

Future screenplay work must preserve all five statements:

1. Reality consists of the Seen and the Unseen.
2. The covenant protects both by maintaining their proper relationship.
3. The Oku family inherit stewardship of a covenant node.
4. Kai breaks alignment rather than releasing a prisoner.
5. The Entity is a manifestation of imbalance, not the cause of evil or the
   purpose of the covenant.
