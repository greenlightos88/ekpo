# EKPO ENTITY HORROR PHYSIOLOGY

Status: LOCKED / FOUNDATIONAL HORROR EXECUTION
Effective: 2026-08-13

This document defines how proximity to the Entity affects perception, bodies, local physical reality, and dramatic escalation.

It does not create a scientific taxonomy for the Entity. These are writers' room execution laws derived from the Seen/Unseen architecture.

## Governing horror principle

The Entity does not merely enter a room.

Its increasing perceptibility makes human perception, nervous systems, and local physical reality increasingly incompatible with one another.

The more sustained and collective the perception, the more coherent the Entity may become in the Seen — while ordinary human reality becomes less coherent around it.

This is not because the Entity literally feeds on attention as energy.

Attention creates relationship and proximity. Multiple observers increase the pressure of translation between Seen and Unseen.

## Perceptual stutter

When the Entity manifests, reality may produce a **perceptual stutter**.

This is not:

- a digital glitch;
- video interference;
- teleportation as a creature ability;
- a conventional jump-cut gimmick.

It is the Seen failing to produce one stable answer for something that does not naturally obey Seen conditions.

During a stutter:

- empty space and occupied space may both feel momentarily valid;
- the Entity may resolve in incompatible positions without traversing the distance between them;
- anatomy may reconfigure between perceptual states;
- foreground/background ordering may become unreliable;
- human actions may be experienced after their physical result;
- a character may find themselves on the floor without experiencing the fall;
- speech or sound may arrive in the wrong temporal order;
- local distance may hold more than one apparent value.

The stutter should feel like perception and physical continuity fracturing together, not like stylish editing.

## Physiological consequence / miasma

The Entity does not emit a literal poison cloud.

The "miasma" is the bodily consequence of a human nervous system attempting to coexist with an impossible translation pressure.

Possible acute responses include:

- metallic taste;
- sudden salivation / nausea / vomiting;
- ear pressure and tinnitus;
- loss of sound localization;
- violent vertigo;
- failed depth perception / binocular convergence;
- proprioceptive mismatch;
- involuntary tremor;
- migraine-like visual artifacts;
- nosebleed or burst ocular capillaries under severe exposure;
- diaphragm / breathing-sequence disruption;
- temporary loss or violent return of sensation in a limb;
- inability to trust where one's own body is in space.

These effects are not standardized superpowers or game mechanics. Different bodies may fail differently.

## Residual wear

The body does not instantly recover when the Entity withdraws.

Aftereffects may persist:

- tremor;
- nausea;
- headache;
- tinnitus;
- fine-motor impairment;
- unstable depth perception;
- sleep disruption;
- autonomic hypervigilance;
- difficulty trusting memory, sequence, or bodily position.

Residual symptoms create a new horror question: is the character still recovering, or has pressure returned?

Accumulated exposure should carry psychological cost.

## Increasing corporeal coherence

As translation pressure increases, the Entity may become increasingly coherent to human perception and may begin producing apparent physical consequences in the Seen:

- structural load without conventional footsteps;
- frames or surfaces flexing;
- dust behaving incorrectly;
- objects cracking under incompatible local conditions;
- articulations apparently occupying space already occupied by a human body.

The Entity should not become a conventional monster that punches, claws, or chases people through ordinary space.

Physical harm can occur because human bodies are forced through incompatible spatial/perceptual states, not because the Entity suddenly behaves like an animal attacker.

## Attention / observer escalation

The brothers do not begin with a complete rule.

They must learn behaviorally that additional awareness can worsen manifestation.

A useful writers' room progression:

1. Kai perceives first and suffers most strongly.
2. Malik and Ade recognize his reaction and become partial observers.
3. the manifestation becomes more coherent as collective attention increases;
4. another family member beginning to notice creates immediate danger;
5. Ade discovers practically that redirecting ordinary people away from the affected space reduces exposure/translation pressure;
6. only later may the brothers develop an intentional strategy of limiting awareness.

Do not let them immediately state or fully understand the metaphysical mechanism.

## Ade's human function

Ade receives no supernatural office.

His social intelligence becomes survival infrastructure.

He can:

- redirect attention without creating panic;
- protect children/relatives from looking toward affected space;
- invent ordinary explanations under pressure;
- recognize when Malik's commands would make everyone look;
- move people through humor, household behavior, lies, or social authority;
- see what supernatural stewardship is doing to his brothers.

This gives the three brothers a collective human mission: protect their family while they learn what their father's silence exposed them to.

## Family-protection layer

The Entity's escalation turns the mystery into an obligation beyond the brothers themselves.

They gradually realize that unmanaged attention and proximity may expose the wider household to the same pressure.

This does not automatically make them stewards.

It gives them an immediate reason to act together:

**their family is now inside the radius of the breach.**

## Sister-death implication

This physiology makes the sister's canonical death mechanically/emotionally legible at writer level without requiring early exposition.

During the earlier catastrophic destabilization, Solomon's procedural failure left his sister confronting severe Unseen pressure without the full procedural protection/support their partnership required.

Her perceptual aptitude would have made her especially capable of recognizing and responding to the disturbance — and especially exposed to reciprocal attention and translation pressure.

She restored equilibrium and died doing so.

Do not reduce her death to "the Entity killed her" in a conventional attack.

The tragedy is that she remained in intolerable contact long enough to restore the relationship between Seen and Unseen while her body and perception were being destroyed by the conditions Solomon failed to help prevent.

The exact on-page reveal remains deferred until it directly changes a present Malik decision.

## Horror quality law

Restraint no longer means withholding consequence.

From Sequence 12 onward, EKPO may become increasingly visceral, surreal, physiological, and psychologically punishing while preserving taste and metaphysical coherence.

The audience should fear not only seeing the Entity, but what seeing it does to the reliability of body, memory, space, sequence, and the people nearby.
