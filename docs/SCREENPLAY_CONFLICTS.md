## Sequence VI — shrine wording

Previous conflict:

Roots were described as appearing to "hold it shut," which could imply prison/cage containment.

Resolution:

The canonical Fountain now states that the roots brace the shrine's seams where stone and earth have shifted against each other.

Status: RESOLVED.

The shrine remains a regulator/alignment point, not a prison.

## Sequence VIII — shared house disturbance

Previous problems:

- depended on an older capability-ladder model;
- sampled voices risked over-mechanizing Entity behavior;
- shadow lag risked becoming a reusable supernatural power;
- decisive physical intervention belonged to Ade rather than advancing Malik's procedural/protective arc;
- reciprocal attention / failed-translation physicality were underdeveloped.

Current resolution:

`THE DISTANCE BETWEEN` now:

- keeps all three brothers physically together;
- starts by forcing acknowledgement of the river event;
- evolves the river's ecological sound-withdrawal grammar;
- makes passage distance perceptually inconsistent;
- reveals a partially perceptible tall articulated presence as failed translation;
- makes the form clearer/closer as Kai sustains attention;
- has Malik physically stop/stabilize Kai and block his line of sight;
- allows the presence to withdraw without being defeated;
- leaves Kai with an experiential inference: it knew he was looking.

Status: RESOLVED / CANONICAL.

## Sequence IX — disclosure / Bode boundary

Previous problems:

- Bode reconstructed the shrine knot with too much procedural familiarity;
- Bode articulated Entity learning rules too definitively;
- scene fed directly into the superseded Bode-maintenance-proxy model.

Current resolution:

`WHAT HE OPENED` now:

- makes Kai provide photographs and disclose the talisman himself;
- lets Bode recognize the dangerous place rather than identify a complete mechanism;
- has Bode explicitly say he does not know what the brass object is;
- keeps Bode to family history, dangerous boundaries, Solomon's changed behavior, and hidden Calabar work;
- does not let Bode explain the Entity, succession, repair, or inner door;
- makes Eno reorganize household safety through ordinary authority;
- makes Eno, not mythology, force access to the room.

Status: RESOLVED / CANONICAL.

## Sequence X — maintenance room

Previous direct contradictions:

- made Bode Solomon's local maintenance proxy;
- implied Solomon directed the work from Canada;
- distributed custodial work across Solomon/Bode/sister;
- used one mixed three-hand notebook as the reveal;
- omitted the inner maintained door/interface.

Current resolution:

`THE TWO RECORDS` now:

- shows evidence Solomon physically returned to Calabar for maintenance after moving to Canada;
- does not make Bode a maintenance proxy or co-custodian;
- establishes the outer household cord as a simple practical closure distinct from regulator binding;
- presents the room as a working spiritual-maintenance environment, not occult spectacle;
- introduces the inner maintained door as measured, bound, unfinished, and part of the same covenant architecture;
- keeps the inner door closed and non-supernatural in behavior;
- reveals Solomon's procedural ledger;
- reveals the sister's perceptual notebooks;
- aligns the two record systems through matching dates;
- lets the sister's handwriting stop while Solomon continues alone;
- gives later Solomon records awkward, tentative perceptual observations;
- places Malik with the procedural record and Kai with the perceptual record while Ade sees the arrangement;
- stops before formal succession, full cosmology, repair, or sister-death causality.

Status: RESOLVED / CANONICAL.
