## What this historical stage accomplished

Before the screenplay itself was rewritten, the repository documentation was synchronized to the final locked mythology:

- Seen / Unseen stewardship replaced containment mythology;
- shrine became a regulator/alignment point rather than a prison;
- Entity became an independent ancient Unseen intelligence attracted to breaches;
- Solomon and his sister became complementary procedural/perceptual stewards;
- succession became capacity + training + voluntary acceptance;
- Bode maintenance-proxy/co-custodian logic was rejected;
- Malik/Kai aptitude was separated from Chosen One logic;
- Ade was locked with no supernatural office;
- Eno remained household/family authority rather than mythology expert;
- sister-death causality was locked at writer level but deferred on-page.

At that intermediate stage, the screenplay was temporarily considered canon-clean only through Sequence VII because the old Sequences VIII-X still contradicted the new architecture.

That condition no longer applies.
