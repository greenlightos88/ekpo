The next sequence should answer **one practical question** and create a sharper one.

1. Stay in morning/daylight long enough for the two-record discovery to create human consequence.
2. Let Malik test the procedural record as a system, not as destiny.
3. Let Kai compare perceptual observations without deliberately feeding the attention loop.
4. Let Ade interrupt any emerging assumption that the two brothers must automatically become replacements for the dead pair.

- why opening is required;
- who understands enough to attempt it;
- what consent exists;
- what practical condition has changed;
- what consequence follows.
