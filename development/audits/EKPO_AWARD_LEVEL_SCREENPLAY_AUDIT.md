## Sequence 11 — `WHAT HE LEFT`

**PASS.**

The scene avoids becoming another mythology briefing.

It dramatizes Solomon's `access without appointment` contradiction through physical evidence:

- future maintenance intervals exist;
- the next interval is nine days overdue;
- materials, measurements, travel history, locations, and keys are organized;
- no person is named to continue the work.

Kai experiences the absence as another form of Solomon's silence. Malik reads the concrete procedural problem. Ade prevents either brother from converting incomplete evidence into a complete interpretation of their father.

The brothers create their own operating rule:

- nobody goes alone;
- nobody decides alone.

This is voluntary collective action without appointing anyone a supernatural role.

## Sequence 12 — `WHEN IT STAYS`

**PASS.**

The sequence cashes the film's accumulated restraint without turning the Entity into a conventional attacking monster.

The accepted horror engine is perceptual/physiological incompatibility:

- metallic taste and pressure;
- delayed/misaligned sound;
- binocular convergence failure;
- violent vertigo/proprioceptive fracture;
- perceptual stutter;
- discontinuity between action and experienced movement;
- breathing-sequence disruption;
- ocular/nasal bleeding;
- salivation/vomiting;
- transient motor/sensory failure;
- apparent structural load;
- residual injury after withdrawal.

The defining visual inversion is that the Entity becomes more perceptually coherent while human bodies and local space become less coherent.
