# EKPO — SEQUENCES 1-7 ELEVATION AUDIT

Date: 2026-08-13
Status: ACTIVE / REWRITE REQUIRED BEFORE PROMOTION
Scope: Sequences 1-7 only
Benchmark: accepted screenplay execution in Sequences 8-12

## Purpose

Bring the opening half of EKPO to the same authored, behavior-first, character-specific and horror-capable standard established by the accepted later pages.

This is not permission to rewrite strong material merely because it is older.

Preserve what is already distinctive. Remove only material that is over-written, over-explained, falsely prophetic, tonally generic, redundant, or inconsistent with the current voice/canon/horror architecture.

## Global verdict

The opening contains a strong movie already. Its best material must be protected:

- the kettle / clock / three coffees / crooked photograph opening;
- Malik's inability to surrender physical responsibility;
- Ade's social intelligence rather than generic comic relief;
- Kai's object/pattern attention;
- the scratched photograph;
- `Give me one side.`;
- the will as request rather than coercive inheritance;
- Calabar arriving as lived ordinary life rather than exotic supernatural geography;
- Eno's household authority and family comedy;
- the red-corded door as a quiet plant;
- the shared carrying of Solomon across the land;
- Kai's voluntary breach of the regulator;
- the river scattering and first shared pressure.

The opening does **not** need more supernatural incidents. It needs better contamination by the later film's rules: bodily specificity, causal motifs, less writerly dialogue, less convenient foreshadowing, and more trust in images.

## Sequence 1 — THE LAST MORNING

### Keep

The domestic object system is excellent and already foreshadows Solomon's procedural nature without explanation. Malik/Ade/Kai are differentiated through behavior before plot begins.

### Elevate

- Compress object inventory where two details accomplish the same work.
- Preserve the clock because later sequence/continuity horror can corrupt Malik's relationship to time.
- Preserve Kai's auditory sensitivity, but make it bodily enough that Sequence 12 feels like an escalation of an established nervous system rather than a new trait.
- Protect `It loses time`, `He hated losing time`, and Malik's procedural responses.
- Audit Ade jokes for density; the strongest jokes reveal relationship, the weaker ones merely decorate.

## Sequence 2 — THE FUNERAL

### Keep

- ordinary microphone feedback overwhelming Kai;
- Ade regulating Kai socially and physically;
- Bode's late arrival;
- Malik/Bode sharing the urn;
- scratched sister photograph.

### Rewrite targets

- `mercy upon what remains unfinished` is too convenient/prophetic and makes the officiant sound like the screenplay. Replace with ordinary funeral language.
- `Your father had many beliefs before he had responsibilities` is polished writer language. Bode should give a smaller human answer.
- `Someone your father loved badly` overstates Bode's thematic understanding and foreshadows the sister relationship too neatly. Replace with a restrained familial identification or refusal.
- Keep `Give me one side.` It is foundational Malik grammar and later stewardship resonance without being supernatural exposition.

## Sequence 3 — THE WILL AND DECISION

### Keep

The will architecture is excellent: land is an undivided gift, ashes are a request, choice remains voluntary.

### Rewrite targets

- Kai's `I want the silence to stop being the only thing we inherit` is too polished and violates the current Kai voice law. Convert the same anger into concrete evidence: photograph, carving, Bode's refusal, Solomon's request.
- Ade's `If Kai goes alone, we become...` speech explains relational geometry that can be made shorter and more Ade-specific.
- Bode's `He did not ask me to choose for you` is canonically correct but slightly declarative; preserve the boundary while reducing the sense that Bode already knows the movie's succession philosophy.
- Preserve the final voluntary `we go together / we stay together` decision.

## Sequence 4 — ARRIVAL

### Verdict

Mostly KEEP.

The sequence correctly makes Calabar ordinary, contemporary and socially specific before the supernatural architecture expands.

### Light polish only

- protect the porter interaction because it immediately displaces Malik's Vancouver competence;
- protect Kai filming ordinary people;
- protect Bode's childhood memories of Solomon;
- avoid adding ominous inserts, folklore warnings, strange villagers, or supernatural foreshadowing here.

## Sequence 5 — HOMECOMING

### Keep

This sequence is one of the opening's strongest assets. The family needs to be worth protecting before Sequence 12 makes that protection a mission.

Protect:

- Eno greeting each brother differently;
- Solomon's urn entering a functioning household;
- red-corded door plant;
- dinner comedy and mango story;
- Malik's involuntary laugh;
- brothers' room aftermath;
- breakfast / eggs callback.

### Rewrite targets

- Eno's `A child can forgive a father. A child does not always forgive a man.` is elegant but risks sounding like authored thesis rather than Eno. Replace with a concrete lived-family observation or let the silence/Bode glance carry more of the burden.
- `I will still be here when you learn the difference` similarly overstates Eno's thematic function.
- Preserve her refusal to explain, but make it household/familial rather than oracle-like.

## Sequence 6 — THE LAND AND THE SHRINE

### Keep

The carrying choreography, boundary stones, ravine discovery, voluntary thread break, talisman removal, spider, and immediate ecological withdrawal are core.

### Major elevation target: Bode warning

Current dialogue about sound traveling badly, water bending it, trees throwing it back, and distance changing under the trees is too perfectly predictive of the Entity's later grammar.

It risks making Bode secretly knowledgeable beyond his locked boundary and makes the later horror feel pre-explained.

Replace with a practical land warning Bode can honestly know: stay where he can see them, unstable washout, old boundary drops, do not follow shortcuts, call if separated. The supernatural distortion should later contaminate an ordinary safety instruction rather than fulfill a spooky warning.

### Shrine physiology

Kai's first contact should seed the later horror physiology without spending Sequence 12 early.

The existing cold brass, jaw lock, tooth-level deep beat and involuntary memory/perceptual fragment are strong. Consider adding only one restrained bodily mismatch already compatible with the locked physiology — e.g. a metallic taste or brief convergence failure — and then let it pass. Do not show the Entity.

### Agency

Protect the fact that Kai almost returns the talisman and consciously chooses not to. His mistake is curiosity plus inherited ignorance, not supernatural coercion.

## Sequence 7 — THE RIVER BOUNDARY

### Verdict

KEEP AS BENCHMARK with only surgical continuity enhancement if required.

The ash scattering, emotional release, ecological subtraction, neck pressure, altered distance and collective bodily lock are already the correct restrained precursor to Sequence 12.

Do not make the Entity more visible here.

Do not add blood, vomiting, major stutter, or corporeal load here. Sequence 12 needs somewhere to escalate.

Potential micro-enhancement only: ensure each brother's reaction is slightly individualized in a way that later physiology can intensify — Malik breath/neck, Kai visual distance, Ade social/humor shutdown.

## Cross-sequence character voice audit

### Malik

Strong overall. Remove rare abstract/theme lines; keep procedure, timing, physical responsibility, exact questions.

### Ade

Strong overall. Reduce joke density where jokes do not diagnose the room. His early humor must remain abundant enough that its disappearance under later terror has value.

### Kai

Needs the most dialogue cleanup in Sequences 1-3. Replace polished thematic conclusions with observed inconsistencies and concrete accusations.

### Bode

Needs the most knowledge-boundary cleanup. Early Bode should feel like a man protecting a wound and practical land knowledge, not someone quietly delivering supernatural rules before the brothers know there are rules.

### Eno

Needs removal of oracle-like aphorisms. Her authority is strongest when she speaks through food, house, kinship, bodies, manners, and what people are doing right now.

## Horror continuity audit

The opening should seed the later physiology, not imitate it.

Allowed early seeds:

- Kai's sensory sensitivity at the funeral;
- shrine: cold brass, metallic taste, tooth/bone pressure, momentary visual mismatch;
- river: environmental subtraction, neck pressure, breath interruption, visual distance instability, Ade's humor disappearing.

Reserve for Sequence 12 and later:

- major perceptual stutter;
- action/result discontinuity;
- severe proprioceptive fracture;
- hemorrhage;
- vomiting;
- motor failure;
- structural load / local physical deformation;
- wider-family observer escalation.

## Cultural/authenticity gate

Do not add pseudo-African occult decoration or generic folklore dialogue to make the opening `scarier`.

Calabar must remain a living home before it becomes the site of supernatural pressure.

Any Efik language, ritual practice, funerary specificity, family etiquette, material culture, or land-history detail added during rewrite remains subject to qualified cultural review before production lock.

## Promotion rule

Do not overwrite the authoritative screenplay from this audit alone.

Write a Sequences 1-7 elevation candidate, integrate it against the locked Sequences 8-12, render the complete PDF, and run the same hostile audit used for the later screenplay.

The test is simple:

> It should become impossible to tell which half of EKPO was written before the project understood exactly what it was.
