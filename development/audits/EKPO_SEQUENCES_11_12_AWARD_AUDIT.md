## 1. Continuity gate — PASS

## 2. Solomon / succession gate — PASS

Sequence 11 dramatizes `access without appointment` without turning it into a speech.

The evidence is concrete:

- Solomon scheduled future maintenance intervals beyond his death;
- he indexed materials, locations, measurements, and keys;
- the next interval is nine days overdue;
- there is no named person, successor, heir, or instruction for his sons.

The screenplay does not claim Solomon knew Malik or Kai would take over.

The scene preserves the double moral truth without verbalizing it as thesis: Solomon preserved access and failed to prepare the living people who would encounter the problem.

## 3. Exposition gate — PASS AFTER REVISION

The first-pass candidate failed this gate in several places.

- `You're building him a defense out of stationery` as an over-written diagnosis;

The audience can reach the Solomon contradiction without being told what to conclude.

## 7. Horror escalation gate — PASS AFTER REVISION

Sequence 12 materially escalates beyond Sequences 7-8.

The Entity no longer produces only environmental withdrawal and unstable distance.

Proximity now creates visceral incompatibility between human nervous systems and local reality:

- metallic taste;
- ear pressure and delayed sound;
- binocular convergence failure;
- violent vestibular error;
- perceptual stutter;
- discontinuity between action and experienced movement;
- breathing-sequence disruption;
- ocular capillary rupture;
- nosebleed;
- salivation/vomiting;
- temporary limb loss and violent sensory return;
- local structural load and impossible dust behavior.

This creates physical consequence without reducing the Entity to a clawing/chasing monster.

## 8. Perceptual-stutter gate — PASS

The stutter is presented as incompatible states of reality rather than digital glitch language.

The scene avoids:

- screen/static effects;
- teleportation terminology;
- conventional jump-scare cutting as an Entity power.

The strongest governing visual remains:

> As human reality becomes less spatially coherent, the Entity becomes the only thing holding one apparent position.

This is surreal, visceral, and specific to EKPO's Seen/Unseen architecture.

## 9. Entity-power inflation gate — PASS AFTER REVISION

The first draft explicitly used a perfect copied Kai voice from the passage.

That risked introducing a new impersonation capability too quickly and drifting toward superseded doppelganger/voice-mimic horror.

The award-pass version removes the audible duplicate voice.

Instead, the child reports that Kai is calling her while the real Kai is visibly incapacitated. The audience cannot cleanly determine whether she heard a voice, perceived pressure relationally, or began resolving Kai in an incompatible location.

The scene therefore creates terror without canonizing a new clean `voice mimic` power.

## 10. Attention-rule gate — PASS AFTER REVISION

The first draft explained that Entity coherence weakened because `one observer has been denied` and that people turning away reduced pressure.

Those explanations were removed.

The award-pass scene only shows:

- additional people beginning to approach;
- Kai urgently asking to get them out;
- Ade/Eno moving family away;
- the frame ceasing to creak;
- Malik's breathing becoming possible;
- Kai's vision settling;
- the Entity becoming less stable.

The audience receives behavioral evidence before the brothers possess a complete rule.

## 11. Family-protection gate — PASS

The brothers acquire a new group-level mission organically:

They are no longer managing only their own fear. Their family is physically inside the breach's radius.

The sequence does not have them announce `we must prevent awareness` as a rule.

Ade simply sees an uninformed child beginning to enter the encounter and acts.

That human decision provides the seed for a later strategy of limiting exposure without prematurely converting it into doctrine.

## 12. Sister-death compatibility gate — PASS

The new physiology makes the sister's writer-level death mechanically legible:

- high perceptual sensitivity would allow her to recognize pressure;
- the same sensitivity would intensify reciprocal exposure;
- Solomon's procedural failure removed support/conditions that should have protected the partnership;
- prolonged contact severe enough to restore equilibrium can now plausibly destroy a body without a conventional Entity attack.

The screenplay does **not** reveal this causal chain in Sequences 11-12.

It remains reserved for the later Malik decision where the truth changes present action.

## 13. Body-horror taste gate — PASS

The bodily fear arises from:

- spatial incompatibility;
- inability to trust proprioception;
- delayed recovery.

This preserves EKPO's intelligence while allowing serious physiological terror.
