# EKPO — RELATIONAL EXPOSURE INTEGRITY AUDIT

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: AUDIT RESULT — NOT YET CANON
Scope: Sequences 6–13, sister-history compatibility, observer mechanics, crowd logic, local-pressure exposure, portability

## Question

Can EKPO adopt a rule in which Entity-specific manifestation is relational rather than broadcast — so the market can contain hundreds of ordinary people without everyone automatically perceiving or suffering the Entity — without breaking earlier screenplay events?

## Verdict

**PASS AFTER WITNESS-THREAD REMOVAL / TWO-LAYER MODEL.**

The proposed model works only if EKPO distinguishes two related but non-identical phenomena:

1. **BREACH / TRANSLATION PRESSURE** — local instability in the Seen–Unseen relationship can affect bodies, sound, environment, distance, and ordinary causality without a person first establishing reciprocal recognition with the Entity.
2. **ENTITY-SPECIFIC RECIPROCAL PERCEPTION** — resolving/attending to the Entity creates a relationship in which the Entity becomes increasingly perceptible to that observer and that observer becomes increasingly exposed to Entity-specific translation pressure.

The screenplay already requires both layers. Treating all supernatural effects as observer-only would contradict Sequence 7 and portions of Sequence 12. Treating all effects as broadcast would contradict Sequence 13.

No canon promotion should occur until this distinction is accepted by the creator.

### Post-removal refinement

The Sequence 12 child witness/voice-anomaly thread has now been removed from the screenplay. That eliminates the only beat that appeared to require a separate third exposure category. The clean model is therefore **two-layer**, not three-layer: local breach/translation pressure plus Entity-specific reciprocal perception.

---

## Candidate model under test

### Layer A — local breach / regulatory pressure

A destabilized regulator can create local Seen–Unseen incompatibility.

This pressure can be experienced by people who have **not** established reciprocal Entity recognition.

Possible manifestations already supported by the screenplay:

- environmental silence;
- insects/birds stopping;
- metallic or bodily warning sensations;
- vertigo;
- altered distance;
- sound-order errors;
- local structural strain;
- secondary perceptual anomalies;
- bodily injury at sufficiently severe pressure.

This is not the same thing as perceiving the Entity.

### Layer B — Entity-specific reciprocal perception

The Entity is not a visual broadcast occupying ordinary space in the way a conventional creature would.

A person can become an Entity observer by attempting to resolve, identify, follow, or sustain attention toward the anomalous presence.

Once reciprocal recognition becomes established:

- Entity-specific perceptibility can deepen;
- renewed contact can become easier;
- the observer may suffer stronger translation pressure than an uninvolved bystander;
- breaking sustained engagement may reduce Entity-specific proximity even when physical distance alone is insufficient.

### Layer C — persistence after recognition

Repeated reciprocal Entity perception can remain consequential away from the original breach location.

This explains the market without establishing:

- teleportation;
- possession;
- omnipresence;
- arbitrary stalking;
- unlimited range;
- automatic effects on crowds.

Exact tether, range, and persistence remain unresolved.

### Layer D — aptitude changes threshold, not eligibility

Kai and Solomon's sister are unusually perceptually sensitive.

That means they can detect/resolve Unseen pressure earlier or more accurately.

It does **not** mean only they can perceive the Entity.

Anyone may potentially cross into reciprocal perception under the right conditions.

Sensitivity is therefore a liability as much as an aptitude: the better a person can perceive across the boundary, the easier sustained reciprocal contact may become.

# Sequence-by-sequence integrity test

## Sequence 6 — THE LAND AND THE SHRINE

### Existing facts

Kai deliberately breaks the red binding and removes the brass talisman.

Immediate effects include:

- environmental silence;
- metallic taste;
- doubled recess;
- involuntary fragmentary images/conditions;
- deep physical beat through root/stone.

### Test result

**PASS.**

This scene does not require Kai to have fully established Entity recognition yet.

The clean interpretation is that removing the talisman destabilizes the regulator and exposes Kai to **breach/translation pressure**. The Entity may detect the instability, but the screenplay does not need to claim that every Sequence 6 sensation is direct Entity perception.

This distinction is useful because it prevents the mythology from retroactively saying the Entity personally caused every abnormality the instant Kai touched the shrine.

## Sequence 7 — THE RIVER BOUNDARY

### Existing facts

All three brothers experience a shared environmental and bodily event:

- insects stop in sequence;
- wind/breeze weakens;
- river surface changes;
- Malik locks and loses breath;
- Ade's social response collapses;
- Kai sees apparent distance change;
- all three respond before any clearly resolved Entity form appears.

Bode is present but does not appear to experience the same degree of subjective disturbance.

### Test result

**PASS ONLY WITH THE TWO-LAYER MODEL.**

An observer-only rule would fail here because Malik and Ade have not yet clearly resolved the Entity.

The event is best treated as **local breach/translation pressure** affecting the brothers at the river boundary after Kai has carried the removed talisman there.

Why the brothers react more strongly than Bode remains legitimately unresolved at this point. Possible contributing conditions include proximity to Kai/talisman, direct involvement in the boundary event, repeated exposure, and individual sensitivity. Do not solve this by declaring bloodline immunity/specialness.

Sequence 7 therefore proves:

> local pressure can affect people before Entity-specific reciprocal recognition is established.

## Sequence 8 — THE DISTANCE BETWEEN

### Existing facts

Kai perceives the spatial interruption first.

As he continues looking:

- altered passage geometry intensifies;
- the interruption acquires bends/anatomical suggestion;
- distance becomes incompatible;
- form becomes more coherent;
- Kai moves toward it without fully registering his own movement.

Malik physically interrupts Kai's sightline and forces eye contact.

The form loses coherence / disappears without conventional departure.

Kai says:

> It kept getting clearer.

### Test result

**STRONG PASS.**

This is the cleanest existing evidence for Entity-specific reciprocal perception.

Sequence 8 already dramatizes:

> sustained attempted resolution increases Entity-specific perceptibility/proximity.

Malik and Ade become **partial participants** by recognizing Kai's response, tracking the anomalous axis, and intervening. The screenplay does not require either brother to see the same fully resolved form Kai sees.

Important restraint:

Do not define a partial observer as a quantified category on-page. It is a writer-level descriptive convenience only.

## Sequence 12 — WHEN IT STAYS

### Existing facts

Kai receives the earliest and strongest Entity-specific warning signs.

Malik recognizes his reaction and attempts to control his sightline.

Ade becomes physiologically affected while orienting to Kai/the affected passage.

A woman carrying plates almost follows Ade's sightline; Ade redirects her before she does.

No uninvolved household member reports an Entity-specific anomaly.

As the household turns and moves away from the back-passage axis:

- doubled edges resolve;
- structural strain eases;
- Malik's breathing resumes more automatically;
- Kai's doubled vision narrows;
- the cord array settles toward baseline;
- Entity coherence diminishes.

At the gate, a boy tries to peer back toward the affected passage. Ade quietly closes the sightline before he sustains attention.

### Test result

**STRONG PASS AFTER WITNESS-THREAD REMOVAL.**

The scene now needs only the two mechanisms already demonstrated elsewhere:

1. Kai is the primary reciprocal observer and suffers the strongest Entity-specific translation pressure.
2. Malik and Ade are already sensitized by prior exposure and become active participants as they orient to Kai and the manifestation, while the wider household remains subject to local breach/translation pressure without automatically becoming Entity observers.

No voice-mimic ability, deliberate lure, possession implication, or separate third exposure category is required.

### Evacuation integrity

The pressure reduction continues to read as **sightline/attention reduction plus removal from the high-pressure local axis**, not simply fewer human bodies in the courtyard.

The current rewrite supports this because Ade redirects a woman's gaze before she commits to the affected axis, the household turns and moves toward the gate, and Ade later blocks a boy from peering back.

## Sequence 13 — THE MARKET

### Existing facts after surgical correction

The market contains many uninvolved people behaving normally.

Kai receives early Entity-specific warning signs.

Ade/Malik recognize his reaction and begin experiencing their own residual/perceptual disturbances.

The Entity resolves for them in negative space between ordinary moving bodies.

Performers cross the same patch of lane without their movement acknowledging what Ade sees.

Ade's money-exchange stutter is explicitly subjective.

The brothers stop looking back / lose the Entity's sightline before symptoms begin to ease.

Physical movement alone does not create immediate recovery.

### Test result

**STRONG PASS AFTER SURGICAL CORRECTIONS.**

The scene now demonstrates the exact distinction needed:

- ordinary proximity does not automatically create Entity perception;
- the Entity is not simply an invisible physical body occupying one coordinate;
- previously exposed observers can re-enter Entity-specific perceptual pressure away from the house;
- breaking sustained engagement matters more than walking a fixed number of steps;
- crowd presence alone is not fuel.

# Solomon's sister compatibility

### Existing writer-level canon

Solomon's sister had strong perceptual aptitude and remained in catastrophic Unseen contact long enough to restore equilibrium after Solomon's procedural failure.

Her body/perception failed under prolonged translation pressure.

### Test result

**STRONG PASS.**

The relational model improves this tragedy.

Her perceptual aptitude does not make her chosen or magically vulnerable by blood. It makes her exceptionally capable of recognizing what is happening — which also means she can sustain the reciprocal contact required to act when others cannot.

Her death therefore remains:

- consequential;
- voluntary in the sense that she remains engaged to restore equilibrium;
- physically destructive through prolonged translation pressure;
- not a conventional monster attack.

Do not describe her as uniquely "marked" by the Entity unless future story evidence earns that conclusion.

# Bode compatibility

Bode can remain near supernatural events without automatically perceiving the Entity because:

- proximity can create local pressure without Entity-specific reciprocal recognition;
- he repeatedly avoids direct attempted resolution;
- his knowledge is historical/social, not perceptual expertise;
- he may notice family reactions or environmental wrongness without resolving the Entity itself.

This preserves his bounded role.

Do not turn his apparent lower reactivity into immunity.

# Bloodline / Chosen-One test

**PASS, with a prohibition.**

The model must explicitly avoid:

- only Okus can perceive the Entity;
- only Solomon's blood can be affected;
- Kai was born marked;
- hereditary immunity;
- hereditary ownership of the Entity relationship.

The Oku family matters because of history, access, knowledge, responsibility, repeated proximity, and inherited consequences — not because the supernatural system recognizes a magical surname.

Perceptual aptitude can cluster in family history without becoming deterministic destiny.

# Crowd logic test

**PASS.**

A crowd does not automatically become a collective observer.

Most market participants are attending to:

- trade;
- money;
- movement;
- music;
- family;
- the masquerade;
- ordinary social space.

They are not attempting to resolve the Entity-specific anomaly the brothers have learned to recognize.

If an uninvolved person notices the brothers' terror, follows their gaze, encounters an anomaly, and persists in trying to understand it, that person **could** cross into reciprocal observation.

Curiosity can therefore recruit a new observer — but it is not instantaneous, automatic, or bloodline-dependent.

# Main rule conflict discovered

The phrase:

> Presence is not exposure.

is **too absolute** and should not be canonized.

Sequence 7 proves that proximity to a destabilized boundary can expose people to local breach/translation pressure before Entity recognition.

A more accurate formulation is:

> **Proximity can expose a person to breach pressure. Proximity alone does not make that person an Entity observer.**

This is the central distinction produced by the audit.

# Candidate rule set that survives Sequences 6–13

The following is an **audit candidate**, not canon:

1. **A breach creates local translation pressure.**
2. **Local translation pressure can affect bodies, environment, and ordinary causality without Entity recognition.**
3. **The Entity is attracted to instability but is not identical to the instability.**
4. **Entity-specific manifestation is relational rather than universally broadcast.**
5. **Attempting to resolve or sustain attention toward the Entity can establish reciprocal recognition.**
6. **Attention creates Entity-specific proximity; repeated reciprocal recognition makes renewed contact easier.**
7. **Breaking sustained Entity-specific engagement can reduce proximity, but physical distance alone is not guaranteed protection.**
8. **Non-observers can still be affected by sufficiently severe local breach/translation pressure without automatically becoming Entity observers.**
9. **Anyone can potentially become an observer; bloodline is not required.**
10. **Perceptual aptitude lowers the threshold for detecting/resolving Unseen pressure but grants neither immunity nor destiny.**
11. **Crowd size alone does not strengthen the Entity. Directed/attempted resolution is what matters.**
12. **At extreme pressure, Entity-specific manifestation and general breach pressure may overlap enough that the distinction becomes dangerous to make in real time — but the writer-level distinction must remain coherent.**

# Required safeguards if this model is later canonized

- Never use "only the brothers can see it."
- Never use "the Entity chooses the Oku bloodline."
- Never let a crowd become immune simply because they are unnamed extras.
- Never let every nearby person automatically hallucinate the same thing.
- Never turn the rule into a game mechanic with numerical exposure levels.
- Never imply that breaking eye contact is a magic shield.
- Never let physical distance become a reliable cure.
- Never add voice-mimicry or deliberate lure behavior without independent setup and payoff.
- Never let Bode's lower reactivity become supernatural immunity.
- Never let the sister's aptitude become prophecy.
