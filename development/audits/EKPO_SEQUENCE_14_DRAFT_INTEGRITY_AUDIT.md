## Sequence

**Sequence 14 — WHAT WAS DUE**

## Governing function

Sequence 14 pays off the explicit Sequence 11 promise to determine what the nine-day overdue interval required before anyone makes another blind decision.

Sequences 12 and 13 interrupt that investigation through escalating consequence. Sequence 14 returns to it after the market proves that simply leaving the Oku house is not sufficient protection.

The sequence shifts the brothers from reactive survival toward bounded investigation without appointing them as stewards, supplying a repair procedure, or escalating immediately into another Entity set piece.

## Core discovery

**PASS.**

The overdue date is revealed as an **inspection interval, not a pre-scripted repair date**.

The revised draft now makes that conclusion visually reconstructable rather than merely explaining it in dialogue. Malik follows completed scheduled dates into companion ledger entries and finds a repeatable pattern:

1. scheduled interval;
2. `CHECKED` / `MEASURED` observations;
3. recorded condition such as `RIVER +11 CM` or `BRASS — CLEAN`;
4. correction only where the observed condition required it;
5. re-measurement after correction.

A separate completed interval records `NO CHANGE` and has no correction following it.

Therefore Kai's conclusion — the date told Solomon **when to look, not what he would find** — is earned by material evidence already established in the screenplay.

The draft does **not** establish what measurement values mean, what thresholds require correction, how the regulator is repaired, or what the overdue inspection would currently reveal.

**PASS.**

The escalation is **agency and responsibility**, not spectacle.
