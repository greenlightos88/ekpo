## Sequence

**Sequence 14 — WHAT WAS DUE**

## Governing function

Sequence 14 pays off the explicit Sequence 11 promise:

> find out what nine days ago was before we make another decision blind.

Sequences 12 and 13 interrupted that investigation through escalating consequence. Sequence 14 returns to it only after the market proves that leaving the house is not sufficient protection.

The sequence converts the brothers from reactive survival toward bounded, consent-based investigation without appointing them as stewards or supplying a repair procedure.

## New dramatic fact under test

The overdue date is revealed as an **inspection interval rather than a pre-scripted repair**.

Solomon scheduled recurring checks, then recorded correction/replacement only when observed measurements required it.

This interpretation is grounded in already-established screenplay evidence:

- a ruled maintenance table with future calculated intervals;
- indexed categories for `BINDING MATERIALS`, `BRASS`, `WESTERN STONES`, `RIVER LEVEL`, and `INNER FRAME`;
- measurements, quantities, storage locations and prior corrected entries;
- completed ledger language such as `WEST BINDING — REPLACED`, `RIVER +11 CM`, `BRASS — CLEAN`;
- Sequence 11's unanswered question about what the overdue date required.

The draft does **not** establish a repair formula.

## Dramatic architecture

**PASS.**

The sequence has one causal line:

market consequence → Eno forces the practical unanswered question → controlled return to maintenance room → evidence that the diagnostic array changed → reconstruction of inspection logic → consent rules for a future observation circuit → Eno prevents injured men from leaving again that day.

No new horror set piece interrupts the consequence of Sequences 12–13.
