## 6. Inspection-before-correction logic

**PASS WITH ONE PROCEDURAL WORDING BLOCKER.**

The evidence chain is substantially stronger than the first draft:

- scheduled interval;
- referenced observation page;
- `CHECKED` / `MEASURED`;
- recorded conditions;
- correction where condition required it;
- re-measurement after correction;
- separate `NO CHANGE` interval with no correction.

This supports the conclusion that the date tells Solomon **when to inspect**, not what repair will be required.

However, Malik currently says:

> `We can repeat the inspection.`
> `Without repeating the repair.`

That overstates character knowledge. The records prove the **logic and categories** of inspection, not the exact safe inspection procedure, measurement method, threshold interpretation, or full sequence Solomon used.

Required correction before integration: Malik may state that they can compare/check the same recorded categories without performing repairs, but he must not claim they can reproduce Solomon’s complete inspection protocol.

## 7. Inner-frame actionability test

**CONDITIONAL PASS — CAUSAL CLARITY REQUIRED.**

The draft places `INNER FRAME` on the non-invasive inspection list while the characters are already physically standing beside the inner frame.

That can create the audience question: **if they already know how to measure it safely, why do they leave without measuring it and postpone the inspection until tomorrow?**

Current scene does not answer that cleanly.

Before integration, one of the following must be made true on-page:

- they do **not** yet know how the inner-frame measurement is taken, so `INNER FRAME` remains unresolved rather than actionable; or
- the prior record points to a specific comparison/reference they do not currently have; or
- Eno interrupts before they begin any measurements and the sequence makes clear that the list is only a plan, not a procedure already available to them.

Do not solve this by adding a repair rule.
