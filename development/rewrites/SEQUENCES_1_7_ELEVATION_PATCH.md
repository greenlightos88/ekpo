# EKPO — SEQUENCES 1-7 SURGICAL ELEVATION PATCH

Status: CANDIDATE / NOT AUTHORITATIVE
Date: 2026-08-13

This patch records only lines/moments that should change. Everything not listed remains protected from rewrite.

The goal is not to make the opening more literary. The goal is to remove places where the screenplay sounds more literary than the people, where Bode knows too much too early, or where later horror rules are pre-explained instead of discovered.

**Cultural-language gate:** Before promotion, apply `docs/CULTURAL_LANGUAGE_REGISTER.md` and `development/rewrites/CULTURAL_LANGUAGE_REGISTER_PASS.md`. Bode, Eno, Eyo and local Calabar speakers must not default to neutral North-American screenplay cadence. Nigerian English / Nigerian Pidgin / Efik switching must follow relationship, status, intimacy and pressure rather than decorative slang.

## Sequence 2 — THE FUNERAL

### A. Officiant

**REMOVE:**

`and we ask for mercy upon what remains unfinished.`

It is false prophecy coming from a neutral officiant.

**REPLACE WITH ordinary funeral language:**

```fountain
OFFICIANT
We return Solomon Oku to the keeping
of those who loved him, and we give
thanks for the life he shared with us.
```

The microphone feedback then interrupts ordinary grief. The horror architecture later earns the idea of unfinished work itself.

### B. Bode / Solomon childhood

**REMOVE:**

`Your father had many beliefs before he had responsibilities.`

**REPLACE:**

```fountain
ADE
Dad said that?

BODE
At sixteen? He said many things.
```

Smaller. Human. Bode does not summarize Solomon's tragedy before we know it.

Cultural register note: Vancouver funeral context and diaspora nephews justify clearer English here. Do not force Pidgin into a solemn Canadian funeral simply to mark Bode as Nigerian.

### C. Scratched photograph

**REMOVE:**

`Someone your father loved badly.`

**REPLACE:**

```fountain
KAI
Who was she?

BODE
My sister.
```

Ade can still ask whether Solomon damaged the photograph. Bode's `Not here` remains useful because it is situational rather than mythological.

This makes the sister a person before she becomes a mystery.

## Sequence 3 — THE WILL AND DECISION

### A. Kai's thematic line

**REMOVE:**

`I want the silence to stop being the only thing we inherit.`

**REPLACE with concrete evidence:**

```fountain
MALIK
You want to fly across the world because
people are withholding information?

KAI
He kept her picture.

Kai taps the scratched face.

KAI
He kept that carving.

Then Solomon's letter.

KAI
And this is the first time he put all
three things in the same place.
```

Kai makes the pattern legible. The audience supplies the theme.

### B. Bode choice boundary

Current version is canonically right but slightly too complete.

**REPLACE:**

```fountain
MALIK
Then decide.

BODE
It is not mine to decide.
```

Nothing more. This preserves voluntary choice without Bode reciting Solomon's philosophy.

### C. Ade's travel argument

**COMPRESS:**

```fountain
MALIK
Then say no.

ADE
And let Kai go by himself?

MALIK
He doesn't have to go.

ADE
You know him.

Kai looks at Ade.

ADE
We go together. We carry Dad to the
river. We ask what we need to ask.
Then we come home together.
```

This is more Ade: people first, logic second.

Keep Malik's final:

`We go together.`

`We stay together.`

That becomes increasingly important as the horror attacks their ability to remain spatially/perceptually together.

## Sequence 5 — HOMECOMING

### A. Dinner / parenthood aphorism

**REMOVE:**

`Children do not know their parents were children. Parents work very hard to keep it that way.`

`Because a child can forgive a father. A child does not always forgive a man.`

These ideas are strong but currently arrive as finished screenplay thesis.

**REPLACE with lived family behavior:**

```fountain
MALIK
He never told us any of this.

Eno looks at the urn.

ENO
Solomon did not like his children laughing at him.

ADE
We would have survived the mangoes.

ENO
Mangoes, yes.
```

Bode and Eno share the same small old glance. Kai catches it.

```fountain
MALIK
What else didn't he tell us?

ENO
Eat while the food is hot.
```

This keeps the buried history present without turning Eno into an oracle.

### B. Land-questions exchange

**REMOVE:**

`you can begin asking the land questions as if it owes you answers.`

`I will still be here when you learn the difference.`

**REPLACE:**

```fountain
ENO
Tomorrow Bode will show you the land.
Ask him what he knows.

KAI
And you?

ENO
I made dinner.

She reaches for his plate.

KAI
I am still eating.

ENO
Then eat.
```

Her refusal is domestic, specific, and stronger because she will not perform mystery for Kai.

### C. Cultural-language pass

Do not convert every Eno line to Pidgin.

Use the register patch for selected teasing/household lines. Eno should sound local through cadence, social authority and selective code-switching, not through slang density.

Candidate alternatives in `development/rewrites/CULTURAL_LANGUAGE_REGISTER_PASS.md` remain provisional pending Nigerian/Calabar/Efik review.

## Sequence 6 — THE LAND AND THE SHRINE

### A. Bode's path warning

**REMOVE entirely:**

`Sound travels badly here. Water bends it. Trees throw it back. If you hear one of us, look for us before you follow.`

`And do not believe everything you think you see. Distance changes quickly under these trees.`

This is the most important opening correction. It currently pre-explains the later Entity grammar and makes Bode look covertly expert.

**REPLACE with practical land knowledge:**

```fountain
BODE
From here, stay where I can see you.

ADE
You say that like we have been freelancing.

BODE
The washout took part of this path last
rain. There are old drops under the vines.

He points to the left fork.

BODE
No shortcut.
```

Now Kai's later departure from the path is a violation of an ordinary safety instruction, and the supernatural corruption belongs to the Entity rather than Bode's warning.

Cultural-register note: clear Nigerian English is appropriate because Bode is giving unfamiliar Canadian nephews a safety instruction. Do not force Pidgin where ambiguity would be irresponsible.

### B. Shrine physiology seed

After:

`The talisman pulls heat from Kai's fingers.`

**ADD only:**

```fountain
Metal floods the back of his tongue.

Kai swallows.

The recess doubles for half a blink.
Then one again.
```

Then continue into the existing palm / red thread / river-water fragment and deep beat.

Do not add more.

This establishes the later physiology as escalation of something already in his body.

### C. Agency

Protect exactly:

- Kai raises the talisman toward the recess;
- Malik calls;
- Kai chooses to close his hand and keep it.

No compulsion language.

# Integration rule

The accepted candidate should feel **less written**, not more rewritten.

Expected result:

- Sequence 1 remains recognizably the same opening.
- Sequence 2 loses false prophecy and writerly Bode lines.
- Sequence 3 preserves voluntary travel while giving Kai/Ade more character-specific reasoning.
- Sequence 4 remains culturally alive and unthreatened.
- Sequence 5 keeps warmth while removing Eno-as-oracle dialogue and gains selective local register.
- Sequence 6 removes Bode's accidental supernatural expertise and seeds one restrained physiological precursor.
- Sequence 7 remains the benchmark restrained contact.

After integration, run a full screenplay read through Sequence 12. If the opening now feels plainer than the later pages, revise again. If it feels more `elevated` because it has acquired extra poetic dialogue, revert.

Then run the cultural-language integrity test:

> Remove the character names and cultural nouns. Do Bode, Eno and Eyo still sound like people formed in Calabar rather than three more North-American screenplay voices?

If not, the pass is not finished.
