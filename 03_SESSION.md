The covenant maintains proper equilibrium between them through stewardship.

The hidden shrine is a regulator/alignment node, not a prison.

The Entity is an independent ancient Unseen intelligence attracted to breaches. The breach does not generate it.

Perception across the boundary is reciprocal:

> Attention creates proximity.
