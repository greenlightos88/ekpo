## North Star

EKPO is a psychological supernatural family drama about inheritance.

- Family is the subject.
- Grief is the doorway.
- Inheritance is the engine.
- The supernatural is the language.

> Reality survives not through power, but through stewardship.

## Foundational cosmology

### The Seen

The material human world: stable matter, sequence, physical distance, bodily identity, ordinary causality.

### The Unseen

A broader metaphysical ecology containing ancestors, spiritual intelligences, dream beings, primordial presences, forces, beings beyond human categories, and phenomena humans may not meaningfully distinguish from beings.

The Unseen is not evil, inferior, or a generic demon realm.

### The covenant

The covenant preserves proper equilibrium between Seen and Unseen through stewardship.

It is not a bargain with the Entity, demonic contract, prison mechanism, or monster-containment pact.

### The shrine / regulator

The hidden shrine is a regulator and point of alignment.

Its red binding, brass talisman, knots, intervals, materials, measurements, environmental observations, and maintenance practices belong to the regulatory system.

Kai destabilized the regulator. He did not release a trapped creature.

## The Entity

The Entity is an independent ancient intelligence native to the Unseen that is naturally attracted to breaches in equilibrium.

The breach does not create it. The breach attracts it.

It is not Solomon's sister, a ghost, demon, god, ruler of the Unseen, literal spider deity, covenant purpose, shrine prisoner, or imbalance-generated symptom.

### Attention rule

> Attention creates proximity.

Perception across the boundary is reciprocal. The characters still do not possess the complete mechanism.

### Failed translation / perceptual stutter

The Entity's perceptible form is not necessarily its true body. The Seen attempts to impose anatomy, continuity, distance, sequence, mass, and spatial coherence on something that does not naturally obey those conditions.

By Sequence 12, failed translation can produce **perceptual stutter**: incompatible apparent states of space/body/sequence can register without a transitional movement.

This is not a digital glitch, teleportation power, or conventional monster movement.

### Physiological translation pressure

Entity proximity can produce bodily incompatibility with local reality.

Established on-page effects include:

- metallic taste;
- ear pressure and temporally misaligned sound;
- binocular convergence failure;
- violent vertigo/proprioceptive mismatch;
- breathing-sequence disruption;
- ocular/nasal bleeding;
- salivation/vomiting;
- tremor;
- transient motor/sensory loss;
- residual symptoms after the Entity withdraws.

The stronger the Entity resolves in the Seen, the less coherent human/local spatial experience may become.

Do not turn this into a quantified power system.

## Two-function stewardship

Solomon and his sister were complementary partners, not competing successors.

### Solomon — procedural / Seen-facing

His function centered on maintenance, alignment, bindings, knots, materials, intervals, measurements, records, environmental conditions, and physical integrity of the regulatory system.

### Solomon's sister — perceptual / Unseen-facing

Her function centered on perceiving Unseen pressure, distinguishing disturbance from noise, interpreting manifestations, tracking environmental change, understanding how attention changes contact, and documenting perceptual phenomena.

Neither function is sufficient alone.

## Succession

Succession is not prophecy, automatic inheritance, or supernatural appointment.

It requires:

1. capacity recognized;
2. knowledge transmitted;
3. responsibility voluntarily accepted.

Blood does not remove agency.

## Solomon's tragedy — access without appointment

Solomon failed his procedural responsibility in young adulthood. His sister restored equilibrium and died. The full causal truth remains writer-level canon and unrevealed on-page.

Afterward Solomon became obsessive, maintained what he could, studied his sister's records, attempted to compensate for her missing function, and failed succession/transmission.

He believed in his children but did not know whether they would accept stewardship.

His final inheritance therefore left **access without appointment**:

- house and land;
- maintenance room;
- procedural records;
- sister's perceptual notebooks;
- material continuity of the work;
- no named successor.

He left them a path and also abandoned them inside a problem he was too frightened to explain.

Both are true.

## Character canon

### Malik Oku

Engine: He fights entropy.

Malik naturally mirrors Solomon's procedural aptitude because of his existing psychology. He is not chosen.

Sequence 11 tests whether his procedural aptitude becomes unilateral control. He chooses not to act blind and accepts Ade's collective decision rule.

Sequence 12 attacks the bodily foundation of Malik's control: his diaphragm fails to initiate breathing and he must consciously restart the sequence.

### Kai Oku

Engine: Pattern recognition driven by attachment hunger.

Kai naturally mirrors his aunt's perceptual aptitude. He is not a Chosen One.

Sequence 11 makes Solomon's missing human instruction concrete for him.

Sequence 12 gives Kai the heaviest exposure: convergence failure, bleeding, vomiting, perceptual stutter, and transient right-arm loss. He recognizes danger to approaching family before he understands the complete observer mechanism.

### Ade Oku

Engine: Humor is emotional CPR.

Ade has no supernatural office.

Sequence 11 makes him essential by stopping Malik and Kai from claiming complete ownership of Solomon's meaning and establishing: nobody goes alone; nobody decides alone.

Sequence 12 converts his social intelligence into protective infrastructure: he redirects a child, blocks her sightline, creates an ordinary gas-leak cover, and helps Eno clear the household.

### Bode Oku

Bode remains a limited family-history witness. He is not co-custodian, maintenance proxy, or mythology expert.

He does not know what the missed interval causes, the complete Entity mechanism, or Solomon's intended successor.

### Auntie Eno

Eno remains household/family authority. She forces food, evacuation, and medical common sense into the story without becoming a cosmology expert.
